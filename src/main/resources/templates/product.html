Product scope

The website product is a guided memoir service platform

It coordinates the family project, not just a brochure site. Adult children manage the journey, parents provide stories, and the system handles transcription, restoration, security, and publishing.

Primary modules

  • Registration and parent connection
  • Progress dashboard
  • Media vault and chapter sorting
  • Transcript review and editing
  • Security panel for private memories
  • Draft approval and publishing

Integration points

  • Google login
  • Facebook login
  • SMTP OTP mail
  • AI transcription service
  • Image restoration service
  • Cloud storage and QR generation
