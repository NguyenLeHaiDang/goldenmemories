Product scope

A guided memoir platform, not just a brochure site.

Adult children manage the project, parents tell their stories, and the system handles onboarding, transcription, restoration, draft approval, and publishing in one MVC flow.

Primary modules

What the product needs to do first

  • Registration, login, OAuth2, and OTP verification
  • Parent connection and project setup
  • Progress dashboard and story timeline
  • Media vault, editor queue, and approval states
  • Cloud archive and QR-based protected memories

Integration points

Services already planned in the stack

  • Google OAuth2 and Facebook OAuth2
  • SMTP mail for OTP delivery
  • AI transcription and cleaning
  • Image restoration and asset organization
  • Cloud storage and QR generation

Built from the plan

Each page should answer one product question

The public site should help a visitor understand the journey, then move them into a consultation or onboarding step with minimal friction.

Landing

Explain the service and send the user to the right next step.

Onboarding

Register, verify email, and connect the parent profile.

Workspace

Track progress, review stories, and manage the memoir draft.

Publishing

Export to print, secure the archive, and complete delivery.
