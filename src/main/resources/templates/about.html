About the build

MVC foundation for a legacy-preservation platform

This site is scaffolded to support the product architecture you outlined: a family onboarding experience, parent storytelling flow, AI services, cloud security, and publishing.

Architecture

The site follows the MVC pattern with controllers for page routing, simple model records for page content, Thymeleaf views for rendering, and static CSS/JS for the client experience.

The next natural step is to connect persistence, user accounts, OAuth sign-in, and OTP mail delivery to live infrastructure once your keys are ready.
